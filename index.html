    Final-year project

    PENTA: League of Legends match prediction

    Predicts the probability that blue side beats red side in professional matches, trained on 60,650 games from 2020–2026. A CatBoost and neural-network ensemble with isotonic calibration, so a 70% prediction really means 70%. SHAP explanations are turned into plain language by an LLM analyst with selectable Coach, Analyst and Betting personas.

    • 64.65%validation accuracy
    • 52.8%majority-class baseline
    • 58,017walk-forward predictions
    • 0.013calibration error (ECE)
